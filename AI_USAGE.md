AI assistance was used during the challenge for:

AI was used to review existing code, explain changes, and assist with implementation and debugging.

During development, I ran project tests and local smoke tests, including backend/frontend development checks and Docker/local environment verification. Only checks I personally executed were treated as completed verification.
